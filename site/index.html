How to use it in your Tesla
  1. Open this page in the Tesla browser and add it to your favourites.
  2. Pick a car, tap START ENGINE and allow location access.
  3. Drive. The engine shifts through the gears with your real speed and acceleration.
  4. Parked? Hold REV to rev it, or use Test drive with the on-screen pedals.
  5. On a computer: ↑/W gas, ↓/S brake, Space rev.
Questions

No sound? Turn up the media volume in the car and check the volume slider in Settings. The browser only allows sound after you tap START ENGINE.

Engine stays at idle while driving? Location access is off. Tap the padlock in the address bar and allow location, then reload.

Does it need internet? Only for the first visit. After that it loads from the car's cache, even without signal.

Is it free? Yes, every car. There are no accounts, no tracking and no server calls: everything runs in your browser.
